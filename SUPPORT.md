# Support and funding

Horizon is funded by donations. There is no paid tier of the software and no
alternative proprietary terms: AGPL-3.0-or-later is the only license under
which it is offered.

## Always free, to everyone

- The full source, under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- Every release and every pre-built artifact, including signed images.
- Every security fix, published to everyone at the same time.
- The documentation.

**No release, build, update or security fix is ever restricted to donors or
sponsors, nor given to them earlier.** This is a standing commitment, not a
description of the current state. The security support period in SECURITY.md
is likewise unconditional: it does not depend on donations.

That line is deliberate. Under the EU Cyber Resilience Act, donation funding
stays outside the regulation's commercial-activity test only so long as
donating is not in practice a condition for access to the software, its
essential functions, or its updates. Gating builds or patches behind
sponsorship would change the project's legal position, not merely its
reputation. See docs/CRA-COMPLIANCE.md.

## Community support

Open an issue. Best effort, no response time, no service commitment.

The software is distributed WITHOUT ANY WARRANTY. See sections 15 to 17 of
LICENSE, and the NOTICE file.

## Vulnerability reports

See SECURITY.md. Sponsors get a faster **first response** (48h against 96h)
because someone is paying for a human to be reachable. Remediation is driven
by severity and never by who reported it: a critical flaw from an anonymous
reporter ships on the same clock as one from a sponsor.

## Deployment assistance

Hands-on help with deployment, onboarding, integration or day-two operations
may be arranged, and may be reserved for people funding the project.

This is a service: it is time, not access. It is not part of the AGPL grant,
it carries no warranty, and not funding the project costs you nothing in
software, updates or fixes.

Contact: shdw <horizon@resurgamus.com>

## Donations

Donations are accepted without the intention of making a profit. They offset
the cost of building and maintaining the project, including the maintainer's
time.

They buy no rights over the project, no influence over its roadmap and no
privileged access. Channels are listed in `.github/FUNDING.yml`.

## Why AGPL and not a permissive license

Horizon is a secrets vault, which is the kind of software most often taken and
offered back as a managed service. AGPL section 13 is what obliges anyone who
does that to publish their changes. Removing the commercial tier does not
change that calculus, so the copyleft stays.
